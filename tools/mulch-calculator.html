Mulch Calculator

Mulch is sold by the cubic yard in bulk or in bags (typically 2 or 3 cubic feet each) — and guessing wrong means a second trip to the garden center or a pile left over. This calculator converts your bed dimensions and target depth into cubic yards, bag counts, and an estimated 2026 cost.

Most landscaping mulch is applied 2–4 inches deep. Too thin and weeds break through; too deep and you can smother plant roots and tree trunks.

How This Calculator Works

Volume (yd³) = Length(ft) × Width(ft) × [Depth(in) ÷ 12] ÷ 27
  1. Convert depth to feet (3 in = 0.25 ft).
  2. Multiply bed length × width × depth in feet = cubic feet of mulch.
  3. Divide by 27 to convert to cubic yards — the unit bulk mulch is sold in.
  4. For bags: divide cubic feet by the bag size (usually 2 cu ft) and round up. One cubic yard ≈ 13.5 two-cubic-foot bags.

Example: A 10 ft × 6 ft bed at 3 in deep: 10 × 6 × 0.25 = 15 cu ft = 0.56 yd³ → 8 bags of 2 cu ft (or half a yard bulk).

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep should mulch be?

2–4 inches is the standard recommendation. Go thinner (about 2 in) over heavy or wet clay soils, and keep mulch 3–6 inches away from tree trunks and plant crowns to prevent rot. Anything over 4 inches can block water and air from reaching roots.

How many bags of mulch equal a yard?

One cubic yard of mulch equals 27 cubic feet, so it takes 13.5 bags of 2 cu ft mulch or 9 bags of 3 cu ft mulch. Bulk delivery is typically cheaper once you need more than about 6–8 bags.

How much does a yard of mulch cover?

At 3 inches deep, one cubic yard covers about 108 square feet (324 sq ft per inch of depth ÷ 3). At 2 inches it covers about 162 sq ft, and at 4 inches about 81 sq ft.

How much does mulch cost in 2026?

Bagged mulch runs about $3.50–$7 per 2 cu ft bag at big-box stores. Bulk mulch costs roughly $30–$65 per cubic yard picked up or delivered depending on distance and type — dyed premium mulch sits at the high end.

Should I buy mulch in bags or bulk?

For small beds (under ~2 yards) bags win on convenience. For larger jobs, bulk delivery is usually 30–50% cheaper per cubic foot and saves hauling. Compare the two totals this calculator shows for your actual bed size.
